Projects and Services Reorganization

Three structural options, presented side by side, plus the phased plan that replaced them for 2026.

Prepared byPeter Biede, Senior Director, Projects and Services
BaselineServices Team Final Org Plan, April 13, 2026
CurrentOption C, as revised September 11

All three options separate the same three fused jobs: technical program leadership, people management, and resource management. They differ in who holds the resource management seat and how account support is organized.

What changed on September 10

Leadership agreed to defer the formal reorganization to the 2027 planning cycle and to run foundation work for the remainder of 2026 instead: roles and responsibilities between project and service management, decision authority for associate managers, associate managers out of the field, invoicing support on the heaviest account, and execution discipline. Option C now carries that phasing. Options A and B are retained as the structural design history.

Option C · 2026 Foundations, 2027 Structure

Current

The phased plan. Five foundation workstreams run through December 2026, led by the services attrition number rather than by the org chart. The structure is retained as the 2027 destination: Justin Yurchak in a dedicated Resource Manager seat under Clint Dollins, one Account Management Group across the portfolio, and performance engineering as a staffed capability. No reporting line, title or compensation moves this year.

Option A · Structural Realignment of Decision Authority

Superseded

The original design. Establishes performance engineering as a named function, consolidates people management under a Senior Services Manager, creates a dedicated Resource Manager seat, and pushes technician ownership to the associate managers. The resourcing seat and the accounts project seat are shown as interchangeable between two named people.

Superseded by Option C. The resourcing seat is no longer interchangeable, and the structural timing moved to 2027.

Option B · Resource Manager with Sungrow Order Intake

Superseded

A variant in which Julia Newhall takes the Resource Manager role while retaining Sungrow order intake, with Justin Yurchak moving to project management on the transferred accounts. Intake is governed by a rule sorting orders into a direct-schedule lane and a commercial lane.

Superseded by Option C. The intake rule shown here was drafted, not adopted. Review of the order record established that every Sungrow job is bid through ServiceIQ with a cost model, so the direct-schedule lane it assumes does not exist on that account.
