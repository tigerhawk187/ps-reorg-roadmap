All three options separate the same three fused jobs: technical program leadership, people management, and resource management. They differ in who holds the planning seat and how OEM account support is organized. Option C is the current proposal; A and B are retained so the reasoning behind the change is visible.

Option C · Planning as a Function, One OEM Account Group

Current

Justin Yurchak takes the Planner and Scheduler seat in operations under Clint Dollins, who keeps final approval. Julia Newhall stays customer-facing on Sungrow inside one OEM Account Group that merges the CATL support model with the Sungrow account. No Senior Project Manager layer; project managers report to the Senior Director directly. Rollout is sequenced by dependency, starting September 15.

Superseded

A variant in which Julia Newhall takes the Planner and Scheduler role while retaining Sungrow order intake, with Justin Yurchak moving to project management on the transferred accounts. Intake is governed by a rule that sorts orders into a direct-schedule lane and a commercial lane.
